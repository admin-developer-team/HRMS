# Public trials and platform support

The apex host (`hrms.ssym.co.in`, or `localhost` in development) serves the PeopleFlow home page. **Start free trial** creates an inactive company workspace and administrator, then queues a one-use activation email. The 30-day trial clock begins when the administrator sets a password through the company subdomain activation link. New companies must authorize Razorpay recurring billing before opening the workspace; the first ₹10 Starter plan payment is due after the trial. The company name, workspace slug, administrator name, and email are required at signup; legal name and time zone can be added during activation or later.

The public `/help` page saves a ticket and outgoing emails in the same database save. SMTP delivery is asynchronous: a mail outage leaves the ticket and queued messages available. Platform support team members with inbox access receive in-app notifications and email for new requests; assignees receive both on assignment. The platform administrator can invite support agents from `/support` → **Team & access** and enable or disable each person's inbox access, including their own. The administrator can re-enable their own access from the team tab. Customer subscription records are managed from `/subscriptions`; Razorpay checkout remains in each tenant's `/billing` page.

## Local walkthrough

1. Start the API and frontend as described in the project README. Database auto-migration applies `SubscriptionBilling` and `PublicTrialsAndSupport` on API startup.
2. Configure platform email in the app's email settings and set its **Application base URL** to `http://localhost:4200` for local testing. The activation email will link to `http://{workspace}.localhost:4200/activate?...`. For production, set the public apex URL instead. Configure the SMTP sender to verify actual delivery; if it is disabled, trial and ticket records still persist and the outbox retries after SMTP is enabled.
3. Open `http://localhost:4200/`, click **Start free trial**, and submit a unique slug. Verify the company appears under the platform administrator's **Customer companies** and **Subscriptions** pages. The trial should have no end date until activation.
4. Open the emailed link on the company subdomain, set a password, then sign in. Verify that the trial end date is about 30 days ahead. The administrator is directed to `/billing` to authorize Razorpay recurring payment. Once authorization is confirmed, the company can use HRMS. See [SUBSCRIPTION-BILLING.md](SUBSCRIPTION-BILLING.md) for the live setup and test sequence.
5. Open `/help` and submit a ticket. Verify the reference in the confirmation page and the ticket in the platform **Support inbox**. Disable SMTP temporarily, submit another ticket, and verify it remains visible in the inbox. Invite a support agent, activate their account, assign a ticket, and verify their notification and queued email.

Public form requests are rate limited per IP. Activation tokens are stored as hashes in the activation table, expire after seven days, and are removed from delivered outbox payloads. Pending trial administrators can request a new email from the signup confirmation page. Keep production SMTP, DNS wildcard records, HTTPS, reverse proxy host forwarding, and Razorpay webhook configuration aligned with the deployment domain.
